# Welcome to Chess Tyro

Everything you need to **learn chess from scratch** - the board, the pieces, the
rules, and the ideas that win games.

## Where to Start

If you're brand new, read the pages in order:

1. **[Overview & Objective](./chess-rules/overview)** - the board, the pieces, and
   the goal of the game.
2. **[How the Pieces Move](./chess-rules/how-the-pieces-move)** - how each piece
   gets around.
3. **[Special Moves](./chess-rules/special-moves)** - castling, en passant, and
   promotion.
4. **[Check, Checkmate & Stalemate](./chess-rules/check-checkmate-stalemate)** - how
   games are won and drawn.
5. **[Reading & Writing Notation](./chess-rules/notation)** - so you can follow any
   chess book or game.

## Ready to Improve

Once you know the rules, level up with:

- **[Basic Tactics](./chess-rules/tactics)** - forks, pins, skewers, and more.
- **[Basic Checkmates](./chess-rules/basic-checkmates)** - deliver mate with a
  queen or rook.
- **[Opening Principles](./chess-rules/opening-principles)** and
  **[Basic Strategy](./chess-rules/basic-strategy)** - start every game well and
  make a plan.

:::tip
Every diagram on this site is a real position - set it up on a board and play
through it to make the ideas stick.
:::
