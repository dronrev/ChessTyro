# The Lucena Position

The **Lucena Position** is the most important *winning* technique in rook endgames.
It shows how to convert an extra pawn when your pawn has reached the 7th rank and
your king is stuck in front of it. The winning method is called **"building a
bridge."**

## The Position

White has a pawn on d7 and the king in front of it on d8. Black's king is cut off on
the e-file, and Black's rook gives checks from the side. It's White to move:

The problem: if the king steps off d8 to let the pawn promote, Black checks the king
endlessly along the files and ranks. The bridge solves this.

## Building the Bridge

The winning plan has three steps:

1. **Rook to the fourth rank** (`Re4`). This prepares a shield three squares in front
   of the pawn.
2. **Walk the king out** toward the rook's side (`Kc7`, `Kc6`…). Black keeps checking.
3. **Block the check with the rook** (`Rc4`). The rook interposes - the "bridge" -
   ending the checks, and the pawn promotes next move.

## Key Ideas

- **Your king must be in front of the pawn**, and the pawn on the 7th rank - that's
  what makes it a Lucena.
- **Cut off the enemy king** by at least one file so it can't help stop the pawn.
- **The rook on the fourth rank** is the whole trick: it shelters your king from the
  side checks so it can escort the pawn home.

:::tip
If you can only memorize one winning endgame, make it Lucena - "build a bridge"
converts countless rook-and-pawn endings.
:::
