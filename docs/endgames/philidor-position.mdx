# The Philidor Position

The **Philidor Position** is the most important *drawing* technique in rook
endgames. When you're a pawn down but your king is in front of the enemy pawn, this
method holds the draw. It's the defensive counterpart to the
[Lucena Position](./lucena-position).

## The Position

the defensive key is Black's **rook on the sixth rank**. It's Black to move:

## The Third-Rank Defense

The drawing plan has two phases:

1. **Hold the sixth rank.** Keep your rook on the rank in front of the pawn (the 6th
   rank here). This stops the enemy king from advancing to support the pawn - without
   the king's help, the pawn can't make progress.
2. **Switch to checks from behind.** The moment the enemy is forced to push the pawn
   to the 6th rank (`e6`), drop your rook to the far end (`Ra1`) and check the king
   from behind. With the pawn no longer shielding it, the king can't escape the
   checks, so it's a draw.

## Key Ideas

- **Get your king in front of the pawn** first - that's what makes the draw possible.
- **Rook on the third rank** (the 6th from White's side) keeps the enemy king out.
- **Once the pawn advances, check from behind** - the further away, the better, so
  the king can never approach your rook.

:::tip
Lucena wins, Philidor draws. If you're a pawn down in a rook endgame, aim for the
Philidor setup - king in front, rook on the third rank - and you'll hold far more
positions than you'd expect.
:::
