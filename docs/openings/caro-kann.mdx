# Caro-Kann Defense

The **Caro-Kann** is one of the most solid replies to `1. e4`. Instead of meeting
the pawn head-on with `1... e5`, Black prepares to challenge the center with `d5`
while keeping a rock-solid pawn structure.

## Main Line

```
1. e4 c6
2. d4 d5
```

It's White to move in the position below - drag the pieces to continue the game:

## Key Ideas

- **Challenge the center safely.** `c6` supports the coming `d5`, striking at
  White's `e4` pawn without weakening Black's position.
- **A healthy structure.** Unlike the French Defense, Black doesn't lock in the
  light-squared bishop - it can develop actively to `f5` or `g4` before playing `e6`.
- **Solid, not passive.** The Caro-Kann trades a little space for a sound, durable
  position that's hard to attack - a favorite of players who like clarity.

## Typical Continuation

The **Classical Variation** is the traditional main line. Step through it, or press
**Play**:

<MoveViewer moves="1. e4 c6 2. d4 d5 3. Nc3 dxe4 4. Nxe4 Bf5" />

Black develops the bishop outside the pawn chain first, then plays `e6` to complete
a comfortable, sturdy setup.

:::tip
Think of the Caro-Kann as the "solid cousin" of the [Italian](./italian-game) e5
lines: same principles - fight for the center and develop - but with an emphasis on
a safe, weakness-free structure.
:::
