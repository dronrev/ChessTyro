# Slav Defense

The **Slav Defense** is one of the most solid and popular ways to meet the
**Queen's Gambit** (`1. d4 d5 2. c4`). Black supports the `d5` pawn with `c6`
instead of `e6`, keeping the position sturdy without shutting in the
light-squared bishop.

## Main Line

```
1. d4 d5
2. c4 c6
```

## Key Ideas

- **Defend d5 without blocking the bishop.** Supporting the center with `c6` (rather
  than `e6`) leaves the light-squared bishop free to develop to `f5` or `g4`.
- **Rock-solid structure.** The Slav is famously hard to break down, which is why
  it's trusted at the very highest levels.
- **A flexible pawn on c4.** Black can later grab the `c4` pawn with `dxc4` and try
  to hold it with `b5`, giving the opening real bite.

## Typical Continuation

The main line develops naturally before Black resolves the central tension. Step
through it, or press **Play**:

<MoveViewer moves="1. d4 d5 2. c4 c6 3. Nf3 Nf6 4. Nc3 dxc4" />

Black captures on `c4`, and after White regains the pawn (usually with `a4` and
`Bxc4`), both sides reach a balanced, well-understood middlegame.

:::tip
The Slav is the `1. d4` counterpart to the [Caro-Kann](./caro-kann): both use `c6`
to build a solid, weakness-free structure while keeping the light-squared bishop
active.
:::
