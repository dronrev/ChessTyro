# Openings Overview

An **opening** is a named sequence of starting moves with a known plan behind it.
Studying openings helps you reach a comfortable middlegame instead of guessing in
the first few moves.

This section takes a closer look at individual openings. If you haven't yet, start
with the general rules of thumb on the
[Opening Principles](/docs/chess-rules/opening-principles) page - control the center,
develop your pieces, and castle early. Everything here builds on those ideas.

## How to Use This Section

Each opening page gives you:

- the **main line** of moves in [algebraic notation](/docs/chess-rules/notation),
- an **interactive board** so you can play through it yourself, and
- the **key ideas** - what each side is trying to achieve.

## Openings in This Section

- **[Italian Game](./italian-game)** - a classic, beginner-friendly `1. e4` opening.
- **[Ruy López](./ruy-lopez)** - the Spanish; a deeper, strategic `1. e4` opening.
- **[Caro-Kann Defense](./caro-kann)** - a solid, reliable defense against `1. e4`.
- **[Slav Defense](./slav-defense)** - a rock-solid answer to the Queen's Gambit (`1. d4`).

More openings will be added over time. If you're just starting out, learn the
Italian Game first, then explore the Ruy López, Caro-Kann, and Slav.
